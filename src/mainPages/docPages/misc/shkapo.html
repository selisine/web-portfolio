A Shkapo is a type of piano that uses hammers to strike 1 of 3 large magically-charged crystals to emit a sound from the opposing side of the crystal. Each of the crystals is equipped with 32 hammers with the leftmost hammer tuned for 32 hz and every hammer after that being tuned for 16 hz more than the prior hammer. This makes the rightmost hammer 776 hz.
