Parser

Type Ictuk letters to convert them into glyphs. Here are some rules:

  • This is not a translator, and the phrases will look different to how they're pronounced and actually written.
  • Use spaces to seperate lines.
    "0kofatuf. 1////fash -1////ta"
  • Numbers represent the line that the glyphs are on, relative to the primary line at 0. Everything below is negative, everything above is positive.
    On the primary line, half-height glyphs automatically can stack. Any number can be put as a line, though, so manual height adjustment is possible.
    "0kofatuf. .5////fash -1////ta"
  • / and | are both considered glyph spaces. They are invisible in the render, but they space out other glyphs. This is important for glyphs on other lines, as if these spaces aren't used, all of the words outside of the primary line will be above the first word.
    / is a normal sized space, which means it will space all glyphs after it by the width of 1 normal sized glyph.
    | is a thin sized space, which means it will space all glyphs after it by the width of 1 thin sized glyph.
    • A thin sized glyph is 1/3 the size of a normal sized glyph. In Ictuk, Zh is the only visible thin sized glyph.
    "0kofatuf. 1//||fash -1//||ta"
  • Normal and thin spacer glyphs are represented as S/ and S| respectively.
  • Colors are an optional feature, and there are 2 default colors: highlighted and normal. I made this primarily for examples where I needed to highlight certain glyphs.
    Normal is indicated with "#n", and highlighted is indicated with "#h". Like line numbers, the most recent color is what the glyphs will be colored.
    "0#hkofa#ntuf. 1////fash"
  • Input is case sensitive; all glyph letters should be in lowercase.
  • All punctuation can be typed as they are in English, except for the quotation mark, which is represented with "Qu".
  • This is still a work in progress.
